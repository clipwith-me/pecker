Pecker

Business Design & Go-To-Market Strategy · v2

1. Executive Summary

Pecker is a B2B SaaS platform that digitalises community incident management — replacing informal WhatsApp/call-based complaint routing with a structured, accountable, data-driven workflow. Residents report infrastructure and safety issues in under 60 seconds; operations teams triage, assign, escalate, and resolve them transparently. A public map gives every community member real-time situational awareness — no login required.

2. Problem & Market Opportunity

Communities — residential estates, university campuses, municipal service areas — lose thousands of hours annually to unstructured incident management:

  • Incidents reported via WhatsApp groups get buried and forgotten within hours
  • No accountability trail — residents have no way to know if their report was ever seen
  • Operations teams have no prioritisation system — urgent issues compete with trivial ones
  • Recurrent hotspots (e.g. a drain that floods every wet season) are never identified or prevented
  • Municipal services face citizen dissatisfaction without the data to justify resource allocation
  • Civic tech apps like SeeClickFix exist but are US-centric, expensive, and not mobile-first for Africa

🌍 Total Addressable Market

Africa alone has 600M+ urban residents across estates, campuses, and cities with a chronic infrastructure management gap. Global smart city management software market: $1.2B by 2028 (CAGR 18%). Community civic tech in emerging markets is virtually uncontested.

🎯 Serviceable Market (Year 1)

Nigeria/West Africa: 5,000+ residential estates, 200+ university campuses, 36 state government infrastructure units. Estimated 50,000 total potential organisation seats. Public map creates a viral discovery channel — no paid acquisition needed for residents.

5. Business Model

💳 SaaS Subscription Tiers

  • Starter — Free: 1 admin, 50 incidents/mo, basic dashboard, public map
  • Community — $29/mo: 5 admins, unlimited incidents, full analytics, CSV export
  • Estate — $99/mo: 20 admins, custom categories, API access, SLA tracking, video evidence
  • Enterprise — Custom: White-label, SSO, dedicated support, on-premise option, multi-city map

📈 Revenue Projections

  • Year 1: 200 orgs × avg $49/mo = ~$118K ARR
  • Year 2: 1,000 orgs × avg $65/mo = ~$780K ARR
  • Year 3: 5,000 orgs + enterprise = ~$5M ARR

Additional revenue streams: SMS notifications, custom reporting packs, white-label licencing, API access fees, integration marketplace

Growth flywheel: Public map (no login) → residents discover incidents in their area → they register to report or upvote → estate management sees resident engagement → upgrades to paid tier for responder tools and analytics. The map is the acquisition channel.

6. Go-To-Market Strategy

Phase 1 — Beachhead (Months 1–6)

Q1–Q2 · Target: Residential Estates, Lagos

Direct Outreach + Pilot Programme

  • Partner with 5 flagship estates as pilot sites (free tier + hands-on onboarding)
  • Seed incidents via /api/seed to demonstrate value on day one
  • Collect before/after data: resolution time, resident satisfaction, complaint volume
  • Word-of-mouth within estate management WhatsApp networks (ironic but effective)
  • LinkedIn thought leadership: "How top Lagos estates cut complaint resolution by 60%"

Phase 2 — Expand (Months 6–18)

Q3–Q6 · Target: Campuses + Municipal

Channel Partnerships + Civic Tech

  • Partner with university facility management associations (NUFFAM Nigeria)
  • Pilot with 2 state government infrastructure units via civic tech programmes
  • Participate in smart city tenders and public procurement processes
  • Onboard 10 reseller partners (facility management consultants)
  • Press: pitch to TechCabal, Techpoint Africa as the "Nigerian SeeClickFix"

Phase 3 — Scale (Months 18–36)

Q7–Q12 · Target: Regional Expansion + Enterprise

Product-Led Growth + White-Label

  • Freemium onboarding funnel — public map drives organic resident signups
  • Public API for integrations with property management software (SAP, Yardi)
  • Expand to Ghana, Kenya, South Africa with localised incident categories
  • White-label offering for large municipalities with custom branding and domains
  • Multi-city public map aggregation as a civic transparency product

8. Why Now & Why Pecker Wins

  • Mobile penetration: 450M+ smartphone users in Africa — communities are ready for mobile-first civic tools today
  • Accountability demand: Residents and citizens increasingly expect transparency and follow-through from service providers
  • No dominant player: The Africa community management software market is fragmented and deeply underserved
  • Public map as viral loop: Anyone can view incidents in their area without signing up — the map itself drives organic discovery and signups
  • Video evidence standard: Pecker is the only community platform that lets residents record video directly in the incident report — raising the quality bar for evidence
  • Anonymous reporting: Lowers the barrier to report sensitive incidents (security threats, neighbour disputes) — growing the incident dataset faster
  • Proven stack, shipped today: Built on Next.js 16, Neon PostgreSQL, Vercel — production-grade infrastructure deployable and scalable immediately
  • Network effects: Each resolved incident visible on the public map builds community trust, driving organic adoption within and across communities
  • Community upvoting: Residents can +1 incidents they're also affected by — surfacing real community priorities without admin effort
